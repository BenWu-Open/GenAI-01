Academic Research Interests

My research operates at the confluence of field biogeography, environmental data science, and remote sensing. Select a topic below to explore core methods and investigation pillars.

Ecology

Alpine Ecosystem Resilience & Tree-Line Dynamics

Investigating microclimatic buffering and thermal tolerance along steep elevation gradients in sub-alpine forests. We examine how shifting winter snowpack alters root moisture and seedling establishment.

Microclimatology Dendrochronology Thermal Sensors

Biogeochemistry

Coastal Wetland Carbon Flux & Sea-Level Dynamics

Quantifying blue carbon storage and methane emissions in intertidal wetlands. We combine sediment vibracoring with automated greenhouse gas flux chambers along fluctuating salinity gradients.

Blue Carbon Sediment Coring GHG Flux

Remote Sensing

UAV Remote Sensing & Arid Vegetation Phenology

Developing automated machine learning pipelines for sub-centimeter canopy 3D point cloud extraction and moisture stress detection using drone LiDAR, multispectral sensors, and RTK GPS networks.

UAV LiDAR Point Cloud 3D Python & QGIS

Community-Engaged Conservation & Bio-Acoustics

Sub-Alpine Forest & Microclimatic Gradient Campaign

Spearheaded a 14-person field team across 2,800m to 3,600m elevations to evaluate tree-line retreat and heat-stress microrefugia. Established 18 off-grid micrometeorological datalogger stations.

Expedition Deliverables

Pacific Coastal Estuary Network
6 Weeks (Spring 2023)
Field Sedimentologist & Boat Captain

Intertidal Blue Carbon & Sediment Core Sampling

Conducted tidal marsh sediment extraction from shallow-draft research zodiacs. Investigated how rising tidal surges alter subterranean organic carbon preservation across dynamic saltmarsh ecotones.

Expedition Deliverables
  • Retrieved 32 deep sediment cores (up to 2.5 meters) in high-salinity muds.
  • Measured diurnal CO2 and CH4 flux cycles using dynamic portable chambers.
  • Trained 4 undergraduate researchers in safe estuarine marine sampling.

Arid Basin UAV LiDAR & Phenological Observatory

Executed systematic autonomous drone LiDAR flights and high-precision RTK surveys over 4,500 hectares of arid shrubland. Mapped micro-topographical water runoff channels and xerophytic shrub biomass.
